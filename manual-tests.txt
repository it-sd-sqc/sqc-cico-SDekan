# Module 6: Ticket 601 Automatically submit after the last digit is entered

## Test steps
1. Build and run CiCo.
2. Type the digits "0000000" (seven zeros) on the keyboard. Do not press Enter.
3. Type one more "0" (the eighth digit). Do not press Enter.
4. Wait for the status panel to close.
5. Type the digits "9999999" (seven nines). Do not press Enter.
6. Type one more "9" (the eighth digit). Do not press Enter.
7. Click the "OK" button on the error panel.
8. Look at the main panel.

## Expected results
* after step 2 the main panel is still displayed with the seven digits in the entry field (nothing is submitted early).
* after step 3 the status panel appears showing "Developer" and "Checked IN" without pressing Enter or clicking a button.
* after step 4 the main panel is displayed with an empty entry field.
* after step 5 the main panel is still displayed with the seven digits in the entry field.
* after step 6 the error panel appears with "Please show your card to staff to validate." without pressing Enter or clicking a button.
* after step 7 the main panel is displayed with an empty entry field.
* after step 8 there is no "Update" button on the main panel.

# Module 6: Ticket 601 Card number is limited to the length of a card

## Test steps
1. Build and run CiCo.
2. Type the digits "00000000" (eight zeros) on the keyboard.
3. Wait for the status panel to close.
4. Type the digits "123456789" (nine digits) on the keyboard.

## Expected results
* after step 2 the status panel appears automatically.
* after step 4 only the first eight digits are accepted; the ninth digit does not show, a beep sound happens, and the error panel appears automatically after the eighth digit.
